# Product

## Register

product

## Users
High-density information power users and efficient professionals. They prioritize speed, clarity, and information density over decorative elements.

## Product Purpose
vTab is a chromium-first "command center" for browser navigation. It transforms bookmarks into a high-performance navigation workspace that minimizes friction and maximizes visual organization.

## Brand Personality
Command Center: Precise, Technical, High-density, Performant.
Think "Raycast" or a "modern professional workspace"—low on fluff, high on utility.

## Anti-references
- SaaS-cliché: Big paddings, sparse layouts, hero-metric templates, "modern" decorative gradients.
- Generic browser start pages: High visual noise, redundant labels (e.g., "URL:", "Title:").
- Mobile-first web apps: Overly large touch targets that sacrifice desktop information density.

## Design Principles
- **Information over Decoration**: Every pixel should serve a purpose. Remove redundant labels and text descriptions.
- **Dense Clarity**: High information density without sacrificing readability through structured visual hierarchy.
- **Expert Precision**: Visual feedback and interactions should feel precise and technical, reflecting a tool for professionals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance.
- Support for keyboard-first navigation.
- High contrast and variable density support.
