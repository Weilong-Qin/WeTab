# Chrome Web Store Listing — WeTab

## English

### Short Description (≤ 132 characters)
A professional bookmark command center for your new tab. Organize, search, validate, and AI-classify bookmarks with a dense, high-performance design.

### Detailed Description

WeTab replaces your browser's new-tab page with a powerful, professional workspace for managing bookmarks. Built for information-dense power users who value speed and clarity.

**📑 Full Bookmark Management**
Create, edit, delete, and move bookmarks and folders directly from the new-tab page. Drag-and-drop support, marquee multi-select, and context menus make organizing your bookmarks effortless.

**🔍 Real-Time Search**
Search across bookmark titles, URLs, and folder paths instantly. Breadcrumb navigation shows your current location in the folder tree.

**🔗 Link Health Validation**
Check if your bookmark URLs are still reachable. Manual validation or scheduled background checks (every 15 minutes to 24 hours). Status badges show verified, offline, or unchecked.

**🤖 AI-Powered Organization (Opt-In)**
Connect any OpenAI-compatible API (OpenAI, Ollama, LM Studio, etc.) and let AI suggest folder classifications for your bookmarks. All suggestions are reviewable before any changes happen — nothing is applied without your explicit approval.

**🎨 Digital Air Design System**
Token-first CSS architecture with OKLCH color spaces produces a polished light/dark theme. Dense layouts with precise typography maximize information density without sacrificing readability.

**🌐 Bilingual**
Full English and Chinese (zh-CN) support. Auto-detects your browser language.

**🔒 Privacy-First**
All data stays on your device. No analytics. No tracking. No data ever sent to the developer. LLM requests only happen when you explicitly trigger them. [Read our privacy policy](https://github.com/weilong/vtab/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)

## 中文

### 简短描述（≤ 132 字符）
将新标签页变成书签指挥中心的管理工具。支持搜索、链接检测和 AI 智能分类，高效专业。

### 详细描述

WeTab 将浏览器的新标签页替换为一个专业、高效的书签管理工作空间。专为追求速度和清晰度的信息密集型用户打造。

**📑 完整书签管理**
直接从新标签页创建、编辑、删除和移动书签及文件夹。支持拖拽排序、框选多选和右键菜单，组织书签从未如此轻松。

**🔍 实时搜索**
即时搜索书签标题、URL 和文件夹路径。面包屑导航清晰显示当前所在的文件夹位置。

**🔗 链接健康检测**
检测书签链接是否仍然可用。支持手动检测和定时后台检测（15 分钟到 24 小时间隔可选）。状态标签显示已验证、已失效或未检测。

**🤖 AI 智能分类（自愿使用）**
连接任意 OpenAI 兼容 API（OpenAI、Ollama、LM Studio 等），让 AI 为书签推荐分类。所有建议均可审核，不经过明确批准不会对书签做任何修改。

**🎨 Digital Air 设计系统**
基于设计令牌的 CSS 架构，OKLCH 色彩空间呈现精致的明暗主题。高密度布局配合精准排版，最大化信息密度而不牺牲可读性。

**🌐 双语支持**
完整的中英文界面支持，自动检测浏览器语言。

**🔒 隐私优先**
所有数据仅存储在本地设备。无分析、无追踪、无数据发送给开发者。AI 请求仅在您明确触发时发送。请阅读我们的[隐私政策](https://github.com/weilong/vtab/blob/main/PRIVACY.md)。
